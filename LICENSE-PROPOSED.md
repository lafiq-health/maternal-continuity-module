# Proposed Licensing Approach

## Status

The Maternal Continuity Module is intended to become an independently reusable open-source component.

The final licence has not yet been selected.

**This document is not an open-source licence and does not grant permission to use, copy, modify or distribute material in this repository.**

A formal licence file will be added before public release.

---

## Intended licensing approach

The current plan is to use:

- an appropriate **OSI-approved open-source licence** for software, schemas and related technical components;
- an appropriate **open-content licence** for documentation and specifications where applicable.

Licensing will be finalised after review of:

- the final technical scope of the open module;
- dependencies introduced during implementation;
- intellectual-property boundaries;
- interoperability requirements;
- contributor requirements;
- any applicable funding commitments.

---

## Software and schema components

Components expected to fall within the software or technical open-source licence include:

- JSON schemas;
- reusable technical definitions;
- reference implementations developed specifically for the open module;
- validation utilities or supporting software added in future;
- non-proprietary interoperability components.

Potential licences under consideration include:

- Apache License 2.0;
- MIT License;
- other suitable OSI-approved licences where justified.

No final selection has been made.

---

## Documentation

Documentation may use a separate open-content licence where appropriate.

This may include:

- module specifications;
- journey-flow documentation;
- continuity-indicator definitions;
- implementation guidance;
- interoperability documentation;
- non-proprietary technical diagrams.

An appropriate Creative Commons licence may be considered before public release.

---

## Pre-existing Lafiq intellectual property

The future open-source licence will apply only to material explicitly included within the defined Maternal Continuity Module.

It will not automatically apply to pre-existing or separately developed Lafiq technology, including:

- proprietary Care Journey orchestration;
- Care Navigator prioritisation and recovery workflows;
- provider-routing and escalation logic;
- provider-network intelligence;
- Care Companion commercial operations;
- Health Intelligence architecture and analytical methodology;
- cross-journey benchmarking;
- machine-learning and AI-assisted navigation systems;
- production infrastructure and internal platform services.

These remain outside the scope of this repository unless explicitly added and licensed in the future.

---

## Third-party components

Any future third-party libraries, standards implementations or dependencies will be reviewed for licence compatibility before inclusion.

Third-party materials will retain their own applicable licences and notices.

---

## Public release

Before the module is publicly released, the repository will include:

1. the selected licence file;
2. clear identification of which materials the licence covers;
3. any required third-party notices;
4. contributor guidance where applicable;
5. updated documentation reflecting the final open-source boundary.

Until then, the repository remains under development and no open-source rights should be inferred solely from its intended future release.
